Editorial Principles

Neutrality

  • Does not persuade visitors to support or oppose any political actor
  • Does not endorse or attack political parties, candidates, or governments
  • Does not tell visitors how to vote
  • Does not present allegations as established facts

Attribution

  • Claims are clearly attributed to their sources
  • Uses phrasing like: "According to the ICC..."
  • Distinguishes between direct quotes and paraphrases
  • Links to original sources when available

Source Priority

  • Primary sources prioritized
  • Official documents and court records
  • Government documents and statements
  • Established journalism
